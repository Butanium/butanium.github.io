Madam or Sir,

We are all of us in the habit of taking it for granted that our personalities do not change. Our hair may change from dark to white, but we remain the same. We have the same tastes and affections. We have even the same tastes in cookery. This is not true. The mind may grow, but the person does not. It is true that the body may change, but the mind does not. It is true that the mind is constantly receiving new impressions, which make it more and more like its surroundings, but the person does not change. It is the body which changes. We are like the house in which we live. It may be repaired, but the house is always the same house. The mind may receive new impressions from new surroundings, but the person does not change.

Yours faithfully,
THE SAME PERSON.
